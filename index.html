FIELD GUIDE / SPECIMEN 001

Small glob.
Big ambitions.

Climb the glowing ledges to the legendary golden booger. Collect pollen. Make questionable life choices.

01 / STICK & CLIMB

VR: Reach for a ledge, wall, or hair. Hold grip and pull your hand down to climb. Two hands, twice the stick.

Desktop: WASD + mouse to move. Tap Space to climb the next nearby ledge, or jump. Hold click / E to stick; right click uses your other hand.

02 / STRETCH & SLING

VR: Keep gripping and pull farther to stretch your mucus. Release a stretched hand to launch toward the hold.

Desktop: Stick, hold Shift to stretch backward, then release the grab to slingshot. Q casts a short elastic thread at the surface you aim at.

03 / TRUST THE NOSE

Inhales lift you. Exhales push you down. Golden motes and a breathing meter show the rhythm. For a sneeze: grab anything and hold on.

04 / NO GLOB LEFT BEHIND

Glowing sanctuary ledges save your progress. R returns to your last sanctuary. Slick blue mucus releases your grip after a moment. In VR, trigger casts a thread; left stick moves, right stick snap turns, A jumps, B returns to safety.
